# Surface: application shell (all screens: gérant, pompiste, client)

Mode: Operate. World "Verre", version 2.0, chosen 2026-11-07 after the user rejected "Registre" (only a recolour) and mock-ups A to E, keeping « un peu E ». User pins: sober, premium, few buttons; Manrope; a blurred background; beautiful animations; buttons like iOS liquid glass, clear and « plus transparent », not coloured; night by default; only the MTG logo (monogram and its blue) must stay. Code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the app is clear glass over a soft world of colour, like Apple Wallet / Santé: calm, premium, colour only where it means something. It refuses the black totem, the flat paper register and coloured buttons.

OWN-WORLD: three blurred fields of colour behind everything (`.ambient`), page colour on `html` only; panels, rail, bars, sheets and buttons in clear liquid glass with a bright rim; the main action and the station's value in MTG-blue glass; fuels green and amber, red for owed or lost, gold for combos. Manrope, titles and figures 800, figures tabular. Corners 14px controls, 24px panels, 28px hero and sheets, 21px squircles. Night by default.

STORY: everyone sees today's prices and where the shift stands; the attendant records a credit in a few taps from glass squircles; the manager reads the station's value on blue glass and today's tiles at a glance.

FIRST VIEWPORT: wide screens: the floating glass rail, « Bonjour » and the date, the hero (Valeur de la station) with four glass shortcuts beneath, « Aujourd’hui » tiles beside. Phones: floating glass top bar, the same column, floating tab bar.

SIGNATURE: the hero on blue glass, opening the addition column sheet; glass squircles with the label beneath; arrivals rising out of a blur, figures counting up, rings filling.

FINISH: tokens and rules in DESIGN.md and `.impeccable/design.json`; the layer is the « version 2.0 » block at the end of `public/css/app.css`.
